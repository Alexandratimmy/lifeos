# LifeOS · Liquid calm

**Tagline:** A little more room to live.

A calm, personal identity with an iOS-style finish. The original orbit becomes a glossy jade sphere with crossing loops, representing personal life and shared responsibilities around one person.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Pearl | #F4F7FB | Base surface |
| Deep ink | #192B35 | Primary text |

| Mist | #DFF2EC | Household accents |

| Lavender | #EAE7FC | Family accents |
| Muted blue-grey | #677A84 | Supporting text |

**Typography:** Manrope Regular, Medium and Bold, bundled locally. Strong headings, familiar sentence case, clear action labels. Font license included under `licenses/`.

**Surfaces:** Pearl gradients, translucent white panels, restrained soft shadows, thin white highlights and a floating blurred navigation bar. Jade anchors the focal assistant panel.

**Motion:** Gentle press springs, short screen entrances, sliding editor sheets and subtle vertical orbit movement. Respect system and in-app reduced-motion preferences. Haptics are native-only and optional.

**Voice:** Warm, direct and specific. No guilt, performance rankings or promises that household members will participate. Distinguish demo states, pending handovers and accepted ownership clearly.

**Accessibility:** State labels accompany color, controls expose roles and selected/checked state, native text scaling remains enabled. Further physical-device large-text and VoiceOver/TalkBack verification is required.

The orbit is an original vector construction for this prototype. Trademark clearance has not been performed.
